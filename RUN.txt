TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- "python: command not found": use python3.
- A test fails after you edited a contract: contracts are immutable by
  design — update the test's expected contract, don't loosen the engine.
- Chain verification fails on your own log: something edited the log
  after writing. That is the tamper-evidence working as designed.
